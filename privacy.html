Privacy Policy

Clash Clips Studio · Last updated 17 November 2026

Clash Clips Studio ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool. Its owner uses it to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information for the signed-in account (display name, avatar, open ID), used to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to upload the videos the owner chooses to post.
  • Posting options TikTok returns for the account (allowed visibility settings, maximum video length) and the status of each upload.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, no database in the cloud, no analytics and no advertising.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, and only when the owner asks the app to post them.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting TikTok in the app, which deletes the stored tokens, or by removing "Clash Clips Studio" under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services in TikTok.

Contact

Questions: message @animeclashdaily on TikTok.
